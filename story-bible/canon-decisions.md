# Canon Decisions — Shadows of the Veil (Terramythica)

Ratified by Kafil (author) in session with Muse, 2026-11-08. These rulings are canon law for all drafting and editing. They override the NOVEL-BLUEPRINT where the Blueprint only proposed them.

## 1. Protagonist's surname — RATIFIED: **Knightley**
- The protagonist is **Damion Knightley**.
- "Knightly" in the drafted prose (luminara-opening.md) and in the cover-art filename is superseded; fix during the editing pass.
- Decided 2026-11-08 (author chose Knightley over the Blueprint's Knightly recommendation).

## 2. Warden of Dread vs. the Nightmare King — RATIFIED: **two separate entities**
- The Warden of Dread and the Nightmare King are NOT the same being. The Blueprint's proposed merge is rejected.
- Drafting consequence: the Warden of Dread is Book One's climax antagonist (confinement/dread; resists the Phoenix Reforged). The Nightmare King stays distinct and largely offstage in Book One — a greater power held in reserve; do not let him overshadow the Warden.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 3. Selene and the Ninefold Flame prophecy — RATIFIED: **Selene removed it, on unknown orders**
- Per Log 65 (the mega-consolidation log): Selene Mykaris removed the Ninefold Flame prophecy from the archives herself, acting on orders from an unnamed source.
- Drafting consequence: Selene is complicit, not merely the discoverer. The live mystery is WHO ordered the removal — do not reveal the order-giver in Book One unless planned; the chain points upward in the Order.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 4. Caelum's dark bargain — RATIFIED: **leave unresolved in Book One**
- The Blueprint's settled Act III reveal is rejected for Book One. Whether High Emissary Caelum Draven actually made a dark bargain stays an open suspicion.
- Drafting consequence: seed the suspicion (rumor, gaps in the record, his protectiveness around the buried prophecy) but never confirm or deny it on-page in Book One. Scenes involving Caelum must be written to work under either truth; the question carries into the sequel.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 5. Age of the Order — RATIFIED: **ancient — thousands of years**
- The Order of the Emblematic is an ancient institution, founded thousands of years ago (deep-time scale, consistent with the parked 10,000-year timeline material), NOT the Blueprint's assumed ~3–4 centuries.
- Drafting consequence: the Order's archives, rituals, schisms and buried secrets carry millennia of weight; it is an institution that has spent thousands of years protecting itself. Selene's buried prophecy is one secret among ages of them.
- Still to reconcile in the History/Timeline pass: the conflicting absolute dates in the files (e.g. 612 A.E. vs. 1250 AE). Until that pass, keep specific calendar dates soft on-page.
- Decided 2026-11-08 (author chose the ancient Order over the split young-Order/ancient-world recommendation).

## 6. Origin of Damion's mark — RATIFIED: **born faint; the forge burn woke it**
- Damion was born with a faint, dormant Phoenix mark. At age seven, the loss-of-control accident at the family forge (the personal-arcs vignette "The Ember That Wouldn't Die") awakened it — searing it into its feather shape, nearly costing him his arm, and leaving the wound he still carries: his strength endangers the people he loves.
- This reconciles the character sheet (born with the wrist mark) with the vignette (age-7 burn, feather mark). Both sources stand.
- Drafting consequence: the Prologue is the age-7 forge scene ("The Ember That Wouldn't Die"), not a birth scene; the Blueprint's birth-as-Prologue reframe is set aside.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 7. Keepers of the Void — RATIFIED: **a separate faction**
- Chapter 10's "Keepers of the Void" are their own faction, distinct from the Umbraeon Circle. The Blueprint's proposed reconciliation (Circle field name; Alderon as suspected Circle plant) is rejected.
- Drafting consequence: Chapter 10 stands as written on this point. The two groups' agendas must stay distinguishable on-page — the Circle is defined by the "Recurrent Veil" doctrine (Emblems as anchors of a prison-Veil); the Keepers of the Void need their own clear line (void/dark-magic antagonists per Chapter 10) and must not blur into the Circle. Alderon's allegiance is NOT settled by this ruling — he remains a council member who has used dark magic ("I've done what was necessary"); do not tie him to either faction without a further author decision.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 8. Currency and calendar — RATIFIED: **adopt both, used lightly**
- The Solireth fiscal system with **Mythmarks** as coinage, and the **Prime Cycle** calendar, are promoted from parked material into primary canon.
- Drafting consequence: use them as lived texture only — a price paid in Mythmarks, a festival placed in the Prime Cycle — dropped in passing and never explained in an exposition lump (per the Blueprint's lore rule). Full system documents stay in worldbuilding/economy-technology and culture-society as reference.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 9. Who leads the Order — RATIFIED: **Caelum leads; Gideon is senior counselor**
- High Emissary **Caelum Draven** is the Order's leader. **Gideon** is his senior counselor/tactician, not a rival head. Early sheets naming Gideon at the top are superseded.
- Drafting consequence: one man at the top — the suspicion around Selene's unknown orders and Caelum's possible bargain lands on a single office. A Council exists beneath/around the High Emissary (Alderon sits on it, per Chapter 10), but it does not rule in his place.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

## 10. Name collisions — RATIFIED: **cleanup package adopted**
- **Elara Wynne** is an early-draft variant of **Elara Velwyn** (Stag Guardian) — same person; "Elara Velwyn" is the only form used going forward.
- **Ellara Myrith** remains a separate character but will be RENAMED before she appears on-page, to end the Elara/Ellara confusion. New name to be proposed by the editor and approved by the author before any scene featuring her is drafted.
- Zane's surname is locked as **Gryndor** (not Gryphor).
- **Malrik Vael** is Book One's corrupt noble. **Malric Thorne** stays in parked material and does not appear in Book One.
- Decided 2026-11-08.

- **Absolute dates:** 612 A.E. vs. 1250 AE conflict remains for the History/Timeline reconciliation pass (see Decision 5).
- **Ellara Myrith's new name:** editor to propose options for author approval (see Decision 10).
